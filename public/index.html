About

Steady work, considered pace.

My name means tide — and it's how I try to work: consistent, unhurried, wearing down hard problems rather than rushing them. I've spent the last several years building full-stack products for small teams, taking projects from a rough idea to something real people rely on every day.

I care most about the parts users never see: clear data models, fast APIs, and code that's still easy to change a year later. The interface is where that care becomes visible.
